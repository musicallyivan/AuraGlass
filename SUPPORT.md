# Support

For usage questions and troubleshooting:

1. Confirm that Spotify and Spicetify are working and up to date.
2. Reapply the theme with `spicetify apply` after selecting AuraGlass.
3. Check the open and closed [GitHub issues](https://github.com/musicallyivan/AuraGlass/issues) for known problems.
4. If the issue persists, [open a bug report](https://github.com/musicallyivan/AuraGlass/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with your platform, Spotify version, Spicetify version, steps, and relevant screenshots or console errors.

Use the feature request form for ideas and improvements. Please do not post account credentials, private playlists, access tokens, or unredacted personal data.
