# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in AuraGlass to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Explain disagreements respectfully and focus on the work.
- Accept feedback and help keep discussions useful and accessible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and other hostile or abusive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/musicallyivan) with a description and relevant links. The maintainer will review reports and take action they consider appropriate, including removing content or restricting participation.

This code of conduct applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
